# Volume 09 — Market Regime Intelligence Layer

**RegimeX — Open-Source Market Intelligence Platform**

---

## 1. Overview & Purpose

Volume 09 establishes the **Regime Intelligence Layer** of RegimeX.

It consumes the canonical regime classifications generated by Volume 08 (and their associated Volume 07 feature observations) and transforms raw regime assignments into **descriptive, explainable historical analytics**.

```text
V07 Feature Pipeline
         ↓
V08 Regime Detector (KMeans Baseline)
         ↓
Regime Assignments (Canonical Labels & Probabilities)
         ↓
V09 Regime Intelligence Layer
    ├── Regime Profiles
    ├── Empirical Frequency Analytics
    ├── Duration & Persistence Properties
    ├── Feature Characteristics
    ├── Regime Ranking
    └── Current Regime Context
```

> **Notice:** V09 provides descriptive regime intelligence and historical analytics on top of V08 detections. It does not forecast transitions or provide trading advice.

## 3. Volume 09 Commit 01 Summary

- **Domain Layer (`domain/`):**
  - Pure Python and Pydantic v2 models: `RegimeAssignment`, `FeatureStatistic`, `RegimeProfile`, `CurrentRegimeContext`, `RegimeHistorySummary`.
  - Protocols: `FeatureStatisticsCalculator`, `DurationAnalyzerProtocol`, `RegimeIntelligenceServiceProtocol`.
  - Domain error hierarchy: `RegimeIntelligenceError`, `InvalidRegimeAssignmentError`, `InvalidRegimeHistoryError`, `InsufficientRegimeDataError`, `UnsupportedRankingMetricError`, `InvalidFeatureStatisticsError`.
  - Zero dependencies on `pandas`, `numpy`, or `scikit-learn`.
- **Infrastructure Layer (`infrastructure/analytics/`):**
  - `FeatureStatisticsCalculatorImpl`: Numerically safe descriptive statistics (mean, median, sample std $ddof=1$, min, max). Missing and non-finite feature values are never replaced with zero. Statistics are computed only from valid finite observations. When insufficient valid observations exist for a statistic, the statistic is represented as unavailable rather than fabricated.
  - `DurationAnalyzerImpl`: Chronological run-length encoding computing run counts, mean, median, min, max durations, and active trailing run lengths in discrete observation units (`duration_observations`).
  - `RegimeProfiler`: Profile construction engine enforcing frequency normalization ($\sum f_k \approx 1.0$) and duration invariants ($\text{min} \le \text{median} \le \text{max}$).
- **Application Layer (`application/service.py`):**
  - `RegimeIntelligenceService`: Application facade providing `build_profiles`, `summarize_history`, `get_current_context`, `rank_regimes`, and the `from_v08_result` integration adapter.
  - Strict validation: UTC timezone awareness, strictly increasing timestamps, duplicate timestamp rejection, and deterministic tie-breaking.
- **Verification:**
  - 55 dedicated unit and integration tests across models, frequency, duration, feature statistics, current context, ranking, determinism, input validation, and architectural boundaries.
  - 610 total passed backend tests (0 failures).

## 4. Architectural Guardrails (Scope Enforcement)

The following components are strictly excluded from Volume 09 Commit 01:
* ❌ No transition probability engine or Markov transition matrices (deferred).
* ❌ No forward-looking transition forecasting.
* ❌ No Gaussian Mixture Models (GMM) or Hidden Markov Models (HMM).
* ❌ No ensemble models or voting meta-classifiers.
* ❌ No risk engine or Value-at-Risk (VaR) calculations.
* ❌ No strategy backtesting or trade execution attribution.
* ❌ No AI assistants or LLM prompt generation.
* ❌ No trading signals, buy/sell recommendations, or investment advice.
* ❌ No subjective or overclaiming economic labels ("bull", "bear", "crash").
* ❌ No public REST API endpoints (deferred).
