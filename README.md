# RegimeX

> **Open-Source Market Intelligence Platform**

RegimeX is an open-source platform for market data intelligence, quantitative feature engineering, market regime detection, regime transition analysis, risk analytics, historical backtesting, and grounded AI-assisted quantitative research.

This repository is in its earliest foundational stage. **V01** establishes the product identity, principles, scope, and decision-record infrastructure before engineering implementation begins.

## What RegimeX Is

RegimeX is **market intelligence and quantitative research infrastructure** — not a trading bot, not a prediction service, not financial advice.

It is designed to help public users, market researchers, quantitative analysts, developers, and open-source contributors understand:

- **What market regime currently exists**
- **How that regime evolved over time**
- **What risks are associated with the current regime**
- **How quantitative strategies behave across different regimes**
