# Product Foundation

**RegimeX — Open-Source Market Intelligence Platform**

---

## Product Vision

RegimeX will become a comprehensive open-source platform that gives quantitative researchers, market analysts, developers, and public users the infrastructure and tooling to:

- **Ingest and normalize market data** across multiple asset classes and providers
- **Engineer quantitative features** from raw market data in a reproducible, auditable way
- **Detect and classify market regimes** using multiple statistical and machine-learning methods
- **Analyze regime transitions** — understanding how markets move between states, what signals precede transitions, and how long regimes persist
- **Compute risk analytics** that are explicitly regime-aware
- **Backtest quantitative strategies** across historical regimes with realistic assumptions
- **Conduct reproducible quantitative research** with versioned data, models, and configurations
- **Access grounded AI-assisted market research** that cites sources, respects uncertainty, and does not fabricate signals

The platform exists to answer a set of foundational questions that practitioners ask constantly:

> **What market regime exists right now?**
> **How did we get here?**
> **What risks does this regime carry?**
> **How have strategies performed across different regimes historically?**

---

## Product Positioning

RegimeX is positioned as:

> **Market intelligence and quantitative research infrastructure.**

It is a platform for understanding markets, not a system for predicting them with false confidence.

### RegimeX IS

- An open-source platform for market data, feature engineering, and regime analysis
- Infrastructure for quantitative researchers and analysts
- A toolkit for understanding market structure and risk
- A foundation for building quantitative strategies with regime awareness
- A community-driven, extensible open-source project

### RegimeX is NOT

- ❌ A guaranteed profit system or trading signal generator
- ❌ A stock-price prediction service
- ❌ Personalized investment advice or financial planning
- ❌ An automated financial advisor
- ❌ A black-box system that hides its assumptions
- ❌ A real-time algorithmic trading platform
- ❌ A proprietary, closed-source service

This positioning is non-negotiable and governs every product and engineering decision.

## Target Users

RegimeX is designed for a range of users at different levels of technical sophistication.

### 1. Public Users

Members of the general public who want to understand:
- What market conditions currently look like
- How markets have historically behaved under similar conditions
- What general risk environment exists

**Interaction mode:** Public web platform (future). No programming required.

### 2. Market Researchers

Analysts, journalists, and researchers studying market behavior, economic cycles, or financial history.

**Interaction mode:** Web platform, research workspace, downloadable datasets (future).

### 3. Quantitative Researchers

PhDs, quants, and sophisticated analysts who want to:
- Run regime detection experiments
- Engineer and validate features
- Backtest strategies with regime-aware risk controls
- Publish reproducible research

**Interaction mode:** Research workspace, Python SDK, Jupyter integration (future).

### 4. Developers

Software engineers integrating RegimeX capabilities into their own systems or building extensions.

**Interaction mode:** API platform, developer SDK, plugin system (future).

### 5. Open-Source Contributors

Community members who want to contribute:
- New regime detection algorithms
- New market data providers
- New quantitative features
- Bug fixes, documentation, tests

**Interaction mode:** GitHub, contributor guides, extension APIs (future).

### 6. Platform Administrators

Organizations or individuals self-hosting RegimeX for internal use.

**Interaction mode:** Self-hosting documentation, configuration management, monitoring (future).

## Core Product Areas

The following define RegimeX's long-term product capabilities. **None of these are implemented in V01.** They are described here to establish a shared understanding of what the platform will eventually become.

---

### Market Discovery

Capabilities for users to explore available markets, asset classes, instruments, and data coverage.

> *Future capability. Not implemented in V01.*

---

### Market Data

Provider-independent ingestion, normalization, and storage of historical and reference market data across supported asset classes.

> *Future capability. Not implemented in V01.*

---

### Feature Intelligence

A library of quantitative features engineered from raw market data — technical indicators, statistical measures, macro overlays, and more — computed in a reproducible, look-ahead-bias-free way.

> *Future capability. Not implemented in V01.*

---

### Regime Detection

Multiple configurable algorithms for detecting market regimes from feature data. Algorithms will be transparent, documented, and auditable. Outputs will include regime labels, confidence estimates, and algorithm metadata.

> *Future capability. Not implemented in V01.*

---

### Regime Intelligence

Analysis of regime history, regime persistence, transition probabilities, and regime-aware market characterization. Users will be able to understand how regimes have evolved over time and what signals have historically preceded transitions.

> *Future capability. Not implemented in V01.*

---

### Risk Analytics

Regime-aware computation of risk metrics including volatility measures, drawdown profiles, tail-risk indicators, correlation dynamics, and stress metrics.

> *Future capability. Not implemented in V01.*

---

### Backtesting

An event-driven backtesting engine capable of evaluating quantitative strategies across historical regimes. Will support realistic transaction costs, slippage assumptions, walk-forward validation, and performance attribution by regime.

> *Future capability. Not implemented in V01.*

---

### Research Workspace

A reproducible research environment for quantitative research — enabling versioned experiments, parameterized notebooks, and auditable analytical pipelines.

> *Future capability. Not implemented in V01.*

---

### AI Research Assistant

A grounded AI assistant for market research that cites evidence, acknowledges uncertainty, and does not fabricate signals or make unsupported predictive claims.

> *Future capability. Not implemented in V01.*

---

### Public Web Platform

A web-based interface providing public users, researchers, and analysts access to market intelligence, regime dashboards, and research outputs without requiring programming skills.

> *Future capability. Not implemented in V01.*

---

### API Platform

A developer-facing API (REST/GraphQL) for programmatic access to market data, features, regime outputs, risk metrics, and research results.

> *Future capability. Not implemented in V01.*

---

### Developer Platform

SDKs, plugin systems, and extension APIs enabling developers to integrate RegimeX capabilities or contribute new algorithms, providers, and features.

> *Future capability. Not implemented in V01.*

---

### Open-Source Community

Contribution infrastructure: contributor guides, code of conduct, community forums, plugin registry, documentation site, release process.

> *Future capability. Not implemented in V01.*
